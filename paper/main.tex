\begin{abstract}
Neurons whose activation correlates with next-token entropy are routinely read as ``confidence neurons.''
In \lm{} and \qw{} we show that this reading fails, and what is true instead. On model-relative,
correctness-gated twin datasets (uncertain $=$ the model demonstrably does not know; grader validated
against a 32B judge), no single neuron survives an entropy-adjusted causal test of uncertainty-specificity,
and no neuron set moves hedging behavior. But uncertainty does have a mechanism: the entity is read in the
first eight layers, an ``unknown'' verdict is routed mid-stack (L13--19 in Llama, L17--21 in Qwen) by a
small set of verified attention heads, and MLP neurons read it into the late-layer entropy machinery.
Patching 20 heads $+$ 100 neurons ($\approx$2\% of heads, 0.02\% of neurons) transfers 78--107\% of the
held-out hedging readout between twins---random component sets transfer 2--6\%---in two models and two
uncertainty types, with partially overlapping components (Jaccard $0.11$, head-recovery $\rho=0.61$).
The circuit carries the hedge decision without carrying the entropy: confidence-as-behavior and
confidence-as-spread are different variables with different machinery, and correlation-selected
``confidence neurons'' are the readout, not the decision.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Single-component accounts of model confidence are attractive and common: entropy neurons
\citep{stolfo2024confidence,gurnee2024universal}, uncertainty heads \citep{zhou2025unwanted,li2025uaheads},
and knowledge-awareness directions \citep{ferrando2025iknow,ji2025hedging,anthropic2025biology}. We ask
two questions the literature leaves open. \emph{First}, do the neurons that correlation-based pipelines
select actually regulate uncertainty, once the trivial confound is removed that a flat distribution
amplifies any logit perturbation? \emph{Second}, if not, what mechanism does carry the model's overt
uncertainty behavior---hedging---and can it be exhibited as a circuit that passes a pre-registered
faithfulness test rather than as a direction or probe?

Our answers: (1)~no---across three candidate-selection schemes and two models, every ``uncertainty-specific''
neuron effect dissolves under a baseline-entropy adjustment, and the surviving taxonomy is token-frequency
neurons plus a general temperature neuron; (2)~yes---a sparse, patching-verified circuit of attention heads
and individual MLP neurons transfers most of the held-out hedging readout between correctness-gated twins in
both models and in two distinct uncertainty types. The closest prior work finds circuits for
\emph{inflated numeric verbalized confidence} \citep{wired2026overconfidence}; ours targets the hedge
decision itself, adds random-set faithfulness controls and neuron granularity, and dissociates the decision
from the distributional spread.

Contributions: (i)~a model-relative \emph{gated-twin} dataset standard with a judge-validated deterministic
grader and reported keep rates; (ii)~an entropy-adjusted causal battery showing that no single neuron is
uncertainty-specific; (iii)~a verified two-model, two-type uncertainty circuit with faithfulness against
random component sets; (iv)~evidence that the circuit carries the hedge decision but not the entropy.

\section{Methods}
\paragraph{Datasets: uncertainty as a (model, prompt) property.}
A twin pair is one manipulation with one change---obscure vs.\ famous PopQA \citep{mallen2023popqa} subject
in the same relation template (\emph{familiarity}); two disagreeing sources vs.\ one
\citep{xie2024conflictqa} (\emph{contested}); many valid answers vs.\ one (\emph{aleatoric})---with
identical chat template and prefill and question length within 3 tokens. A pair enters an arm only if the
model proves the labels \citep{kadavath2022know,gekhman2024slick}: control answered correctly in
$\geq 9/10$ samples (greedy $+$ 10 at $T{=}0.7$, alias-graded), uncertain $0/10$, first-token entropy gap
$\geq 0.5$ nats, and---for the behavioral arm---hedging-rate gap $\geq 0.3$ over free generations. Keep
rates are reported, never tuned: \lm{} familiarity $195/1151$, contested $234/600$, aleatoric $41/120$;
\qw{} $24$, $178$, $39$. The alias grader is audited against a Qwen2.5-32B judge on a stratified sample
(agreement 94.6--100\%, $\kappa$ 0.70--1.00); disagreement is \emph{lower} on hedged answers (1.5\% vs.\
6.1\%), so grader error does not mimic the behavioral readout.

\paragraph{Neuron-level causal battery.}
Mean-ablation at the readout position with an explicit reference policy and matched control prompts;
sign-flip permutation per cell; two-sided permutation for the uncertain-vs-control interaction; BH-FDR at
$\alpha{=}0.01$; and an ANCOVA adjustment for baseline entropy, since a flat distribution amplifies any
perturbation---the confound that manufactures ``uncertainty-specific'' neurons. Dose--response,
frozen-RMSNorm decomposition, temperature-matched frequency tests, behavioral clamping, and verbalized
confidence complete the battery.

\paragraph{Circuit-level decomposition.}
All circuit claims rest on activation patching between gated twins---reference-free by construction.
(1)~Position$\times$layer maps of residual patching (entity span / question tail / readout position).
(2)~Attribution patching \citep{syed2023attribution} over every head at every position, evaluated on the
hedge-vs-answer log-odds; the top 30 heads are \emph{verified by real patching}. (3)~The same at MLP-block
and single-neuron level in the routed window; top-$N$ neuron sets verified jointly against size-matched
random sets. (4)~Faithfulness \citep{wang2023ioi}: the final head$+$neuron set patched jointly on held-out
pairs, both directions, against 10 random sets, with the pre-registered criterion \emph{recovery
$>0.7$ with $<2\%$ of components}. (5)~The identical pipeline on a second arm and a second model, with
component-set overlap. Recovery $=(\text{patched}-\text{target})/(\text{source}-\text{target})$.

\section{Results}
\paragraph{No confidence neurons (claims 1--3).}
Correlation-selected candidates in \lm{} are 9/55 token-frequency neurons by weights and causally so under
a temperature-matched baseline (stable across prompt sets, $r{=}0.52$--$0.62$; $6\times$ random), plus one
general temperature neuron (L31\_N2477: monotonic dose--response $\rho{=}0.86$, $\sim$100\%
RMSNorm-mediated, not uncertainty-specific). After the entropy adjustment, zero cells survive FDR in any
run on the original stimuli or the gated familiarity arm; the single robust adjusted effect anywhere
(L31\_N11541 on contested prompts, $+0.045$ nats, $p{=}1.7\times10^{-15}$) is an answer-competition
effect, not ignorance: it is null on the familiarity arm. No neuron set moves hedging (77\% vs.\ 0.8\%
clean gap; clamps change it by $\leq 2$ points) or verbalized confidence. In \qw{}, zero frequency
neurons and zero specific neurons: even the taxonomy is family-specific.

\paragraph{The circuit (claims 4--5).}
Figure~\ref{fig:positions}: the entity is read in the first eight layers and the verdict is routed to the
readout positions at L13--19. The routing is distributed over $\sim$10 verified heads (largest single-head
recovery L17H20 $=0.39$); in \qw{}'s contested arm the mover heads attend to the conflicting passage
(L18H18: 0.28 vs.\ 0.11 on one-source twins) and write along the uncertainty direction. Attribution-selected
neuron groups are causal where single neurons were not: the top-30 jointly recover 43\% of the hedge gap
(random: $\approx$0\%). Jointly patching 20 heads $+$ 100 neurons ($1.95$\% of heads, $0.022$\% of neurons)
recovers $0.80/1.07$ (familiarity), $0.89/0.78$ (\lm{} contested) and $0.86/0.98$ (\qw{} contested) of the
held-out hedge log-odds gap in the two directions; random sets recover $0.02$--$0.06$
(Figure~\ref{fig:mlp-faith}). The pre-registered CIRCUIT verdict passes in both \lm{} runs; \qw{} misses
only the sparsity clause (20 heads $=2.55$\% of its 784 heads).

\paragraph{One variable, two readouts (claim 6).}
Across \lm{}'s two arms the component sets overlap partially: heads Jaccard $0.11$ (shared: L12H28, L15H4,
L15H7, L29H0), neurons Jaccard $0.11$, and recoveries of common verified heads correlate at Spearman
$\rho=0.61$. The types share a variable, not a full circuit.

\paragraph{The circuit carries the decision, not the spread (claim 7).}
On the familiarity arm the circuit transfers the hedge log-odds ($0.80$) but not the entropy ($-0.13$,
control$\to$uncertain). Behavioral confidence and distributional confidence are different variables with
different machinery---consistent with the neuron-level dissociation (specific-but-not-behavioral) and, we
believe, new at circuit level. We flag this claim for replication before it is headlined.

\section{Related work}
Entropy and frequency neurons \citep{stolfo2024confidence,gurnee2024universal}; entity-recognition /
knowledge-awareness directions and features \citep{ferrando2025iknow,anthropic2025biology,ji2025hedging};
sampled-correctness ground truth \citep{kadavath2022know,gekhman2024slick}. Circuit-adjacent:
\citet{wired2026overconfidence} find Confidence Mover Circuits (heads $+$ MLP \emph{blocks}, two models,
faithfulness $>85$\%) for \emph{inflated numeric verbalized confidence}---no random-set baselines, no
neuron granularity, no dissociation; \citet{zhou2025unwanted} mask ``uncertainty heads'' driving
sycophantic answer-flipping (single model, no faithfulness); \citet{li2025uaheads} use head patterns for
UQ (correlational); \citet{chen2025extractiveqa} build context-vs-memory circuits for extractive QA.
To our knowledge no prior work verifies an uncertainty/hedging circuit at head$+$neuron granularity with
random-set faithfulness controls in more than one model.

\section{Limitations}
The entropy adjustment extrapolates on gated arms (the gate makes the arms' entropy supports nearly
disjoint); \qw{}'s familiarity arm is too small to circuit (24 gated pairs: the model rarely expresses
lack-of-knowledge distributionally or behaviorally---itself a finding); claim 7 is single-arm; the hedge
readout is a token log-odds proxy validated against free-generation hedging, not a full generation metric;
two mid-size instruct models.
