\begin{abstract}
Claims about uncertainty mechanisms in language models are only as good as the datasets that define
``uncertain'' and the counterfactuals that test them. We contribute a \emph{model-relative gated-twin}
standard: minimal prompt pairs kept only when the model itself proves the labels by sampled correctness,
distributional spread, and (for behavioral arms) hedging, with keep rates reported and the deterministic
grader audited against a 32B judge (agreement 94.6--100\%, and \emph{lower} disagreement on hedged
answers). On this footing we re-examine ``confidence neurons'' in \lm{} and \qw{}: correlation-selected
neurons decompose into token-frequency neurons and a general temperature neuron; on the behavioral
(familiarity) arm no single neuron survives an uncertainty-specificity test, and the only robust
single-neuron effects anywhere (two neurons, contested-source arm) pattern as answer-competition rather
than knowledge awareness. The hedge decision itself is carried by a small patched set: the entity is read
in the first eight layers, a verdict is routed mid-stack (L13--19 / L17--21) by verified attention heads,
and joint patching of 20 heads $+$ 100 MLP neurons transfers 78--107\% of the held-out hedge readout
between twins (random sets: 2--6\%) in three of four model$\times$arm cells, with partial cross-type
overlap (Jaccard 0.11, head-recovery $\rho{=}0.61$). We report what our own criteria could not decide:
confidence intervals graze or cross the sparsity-faithfulness bar in two of three cells, and the
entropy-adjustment used to retire single-neuron claims is unidentified on gated arms; a follow-up decided
the minimality question --- LOO-ranked pruning saturates at 20--40 components, while a rank-1 direction
patch falls short of the component set (and fails outright for injecting the contested verdict). One finding survives on the full table: in
\lm{}, injecting the unknown signal transfers the hedge decision with little of the entropy, while removing
it transfers both. All data, gates, audits, and per-claim evidence
ship with the repository.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Single-component accounts of model confidence are attractive and common: entropy neurons
\citep{stolfo2024confidence,gurnee2024universal}, uncertainty heads \citep{roy2025unwanted,vazhentsev2026heads},
knowledge-awareness directions \citep{ferrando2025iknow,ji2025hedging,anthropic2025biology}, and, most
directly, circuit-level accounts: \citet{teplica2025sciurus} show across eight models that uncertainty is
produced in the same circuits responsible for answer factuality, and \citet{zhao2026wired} localize
Confidence Mover components for inflated verbalized confidence. What this literature does not yet supply
is (a)~a dataset standard under which ``the model is uncertain here'' is a checked property of the
(model, prompt) pair rather than an annotation, and (b)~a discipline for saying when a proposed mechanism
claim \emph{fails} its own test. We contribute both, and apply them.

Concretely: (1)~a gated-twin dataset standard with judge-validated grading and reported keep rates;
(2)~on that footing, an entropy-adjusted causal battery showing that single-neuron ``confidence''
claims do not survive on behavioral uncertainty, and a decomposition of what correlation actually
selects; (3)~a twin-patching circuit analysis at head$+$neuron granularity with faithfulness measured
against size-matched random component sets, replicated across two models and two uncertainty types;
(4)~an explicit account of what our criteria could not decide --- including where our own confidence
intervals cross our own bar.

\section{Methods}
\paragraph{Datasets: uncertainty as a (model, prompt) property.}
A twin pair is one manipulation with one change---obscure vs.\ famous PopQA \citep{mallen2023popqa} subject
in the same relation template (\emph{familiarity}); two disagreeing sources vs.\ one
\citep{xie2024conflictqa} (\emph{contested}); many valid answers vs.\ one (\emph{aleatoric})---with
identical chat template and prefill and question length within 3 tokens. A pair enters an arm only if the
model proves the labels \citep{kadavath2022know,gekhman2024slick}: control answered correctly in
$\geq 9/10$ samples (greedy $+$ 10 at $T{=}0.7$, alias-graded), uncertain $0/10$, first-token entropy gap
$\geq 0.5$ nats, and---for the behavioral arm---hedging-rate gap $\geq 0.3$ over free generations. Keep
rates are reported, never tuned: \lm{} familiarity $195/1151$, contested $234/600$, aleatoric $41/120$;
\qw{} $24$, $178$, $39$; a fourth (situated) arm is dropped at $3/600$ with the reason on record. The
gate thresholds were fixed before any gating ran. The alias grader is audited against a Qwen2.5-32B judge
on a stratified sample (agreement 94.6--100\%, $\kappa$ 0.70--1.00); disagreement is \emph{lower} on
hedged answers (1.5\% vs.\ 6.1\%), so grader error does not mimic the behavioral readout.

\paragraph{Neuron-level causal battery.}
Mean-ablation at the readout position with an explicit reference policy and matched control prompts;
sign-flip permutation per cell; two-sided permutation for the uncertain-vs-control interaction; BH-FDR at
$\alpha{=}0.01$; plus adjustments for baseline entropy, motivated by the fact that a flat distribution
amplifies the \emph{magnitude} of any perturbation ($\rho(H,|\Delta H|){=}0.25$ on our data). We report a
caveat up front: the signed shift is uncorrelated with baseline entropy ($\rho{=}{-}0.001$), and on gated
arms the two conditions' entropy supports are disjoint, so the ANCOVA coefficient there is extrapolation;
on gated arms we therefore lean on the temperature-matched design (below) rather than the adjustment.
Dose--response, frozen-RMSNorm decomposition, temperature-matched frequency tests, behavioral clamping,
and verbalized confidence complete the battery.

\paragraph{Circuit-level decomposition.}
All circuit claims rest on activation patching between gated twins---reference-free by construction.
(1)~Position$\times$layer maps of residual patching. (2)~Attribution patching \citep{syed2023attribution}
over every head at every position on the hedge-vs-answer log-odds; the top 30 heads \emph{verified by
real patching}. (3)~The same at MLP-block and single-neuron level; top-$N$ neuron sets verified jointly
against size-matched random sets. (4)~Faithfulness \citep{wang2023ioi}: the final head$+$neuron set
patched jointly on held-out pairs, both directions, against 10 random sets, with the criterion
\emph{recovery $>0.7$ with $<2\%$ of components}---fixed in the analysis code before the held-out runs,
but not independently registered, and we treat it accordingly (\S\ref{sec:undecided}).
(5)~The identical pipeline on a second arm and model, with component-set overlap. Recovery
$=(\text{patched}-\text{target})/(\text{source}-\text{target})$.

\section{Results}
\paragraph{What correlation selects is not uncertainty (behavioral arm).}
Correlation-selected candidates in \lm{} are 9/55 token-frequency neurons by weights and causally so under
a temperature-matched baseline (stable across prompt sets, $r{=}0.52$--$0.62$; $6\times$ random), plus one
general temperature neuron (L31\_N2477: monotonic dose--response $\rho{=}0.86$, $\sim$100\%
RMSNorm-mediated, not uncertainty-specific)---consistent with \citet{du2025posttraining}, who report that
post-training confidence differences cannot be attributed to entropy neurons. On the original stimuli
(overlapping entropy supports; three adjustment schemes agree) and on the gated familiarity arm, no neuron
passes the specificity test. The two neurons that do survive all specifications---L31\_N11541
($+0.045$ nats adjusted, $p{=}1.7{\times}10^{-15}$) and the frequency neuron L30\_N1457, both on the
contested arm only---pattern as answer-competition effects: both are null on the familiarity arm where the
model actually hedges. No neuron set moves hedging (77\% vs.\ 0.8\% clean gap; clamps change it by $\leq 2$
points) or verbalized confidence. In \qw{}: zero frequency neurons, zero specific neurons.

\paragraph{The patched set that carries the hedge readout.}
Figure~\ref{fig:positions}: the entity is read in the first eight layers and the verdict is routed to the
readout positions at L13--19, distributed over $\sim$10 verified heads (largest single-head recovery
L17H20 $=0.39$); in \qw{}'s contested arm the mover heads attend to the conflicting passage (L18H18: 0.28
vs.\ 0.11) and write along the uncertainty direction. Attribution-selected neuron groups are causal where
single neurons were not. Jointly patching 20 heads $+$ 100 neurons ($1.95$\% of heads, $0.022$\% of
neurons) recovers, with 95\% CIs: familiarity $0.80\,[0.70, 0.90]$ (reverse $1.07\,[0.96,1.17]$); \lm{}
contested $0.89\,[0.69, 1.09]$ ($0.78\,[0.65,0.90]$); \qw{} contested $0.86\,[0.77, 0.95]$
($0.98\,[0.89,1.07]$). Random sets recover $0.02$--$0.06$. Both cross-model cells are the contested arm,
where free-generation hedging is $\approx$0 and the log-odds readout is a token-level proxy; \qw{}'s
familiarity arm is not runnable (24 gated pairs). Cross-type overlap in \lm{}: heads Jaccard $0.11$
(shared: L12H28, L15H4, L15H7, L29H0), neurons Jaccard $0.11$, head-recovery Spearman $\rho{=}0.61$ ---
the types share a variable, not a full circuit.

\paragraph{The decision and the spread transfer asymmetrically by intervention direction.}
Across all four \lm{} cells the hedge log-odds transfer is high ($0.78$--$1.07$), but the entropy transfer
splits cleanly by direction: \emph{injecting} the unknown signal (control$\to$uncertain) moves the
decision while moving little of the distributional spread ($-0.13$ familiarity, $+0.33$ contested), whereas
\emph{removing} it (uncertain$\to$control) moves both ($+0.45$, $+0.55$). This is a directional
asymmetry, not a general behavioral/distributional dissociation (steering the familiarity direction moves
log-odds and entropy together, and \qw{} shows no asymmetry: $+0.83$/$+0.73$); we state it as such, on the
full table rather than a selected cell.

\paragraph{The direction carries the spread; the components carry the decision.}
Rank-1 direction patching dissects the asymmetry further: injecting the contested verdict through the
direction alone moves \emph{entropy} substantially ($0.57$) while moving the hedge log-odds not at all
($-0.10$); the full component set does the opposite ($0.89$ log-odds, $0.33$ entropy). On familiarity the
same split is graded (rank-1: $0.46$ log-odds / $0.36$ entropy; set: $0.80$ / $-0.13$). The mean-difference
direction is thus primarily a \emph{spread} channel, and the decision travels through component-specific
structure the direction does not capture.

\section{What our own criteria could not decide}
\label{sec:undecided}
This venue asks what it would take for interpretability claims to fail. Three of ours are undecided by our
own tests, and we say so rather than round up.
\textbf{(a) Sparse circuit vs.\ distributed direction --- raised, then decided by follow-up.} The
faithfulness criterion (recovery $>0.7$, $<2\%$ of components) is met at the point estimate in the two
\lm{} cells, but the 95\% CI includes values below 0.7 in \lm{}-contested and touches it in familiarity,
and attribution-rank-nested neuron sets did not saturate (0.23--0.65 at $N{=}10$--$300$). Two follow-up
experiments decided the question. \emph{Rank-1 direction patching} (swapping only the per-layer
uncertainty-direction projection, all positions) recovers $0.46\,[0.35,0.58]$ on familiarity
(component set: $0.80\,[0.70,0.90]$; disjoint CIs) and $-0.10\,[-0.32,0.12]$ for injecting the contested
verdict (set: $0.89$), while \emph{removing} the verdict rank-1 recovers $0.81$--$0.97$. \emph{LOO-ranked
greedy pruning} saturates: 20 components reach $0.71$ on familiarity (plateau $0.85$--$0.89$ by
$k{=}60$--$100$), 40 reach $0.91$ on contested---the earlier non-saturation was an artifact of
attribution-rank nesting. Verdict: a sparse circuit (20--40 components) with a directional sub-channel;
the components carry more than the direction, most visibly on the inject side.
\textbf{(b) Scope of the direction-dependent asymmetry.} The asymmetry reported in the Results (entropy
transfer depends on intervention direction) is a \lm{} phenomenon: in \qw{}'s contested cell the two
directions transfer entropy alike ($+0.83$ / $+0.73$). One model, two arms; whether the asymmetry tracks
the model family or the presence of a behavioral hedging repertoire is undecided.
\textbf{(c) The entropy adjustment.} It retired the single-neuron claims, but on gated arms it is
unidentified (disjoint supports), and the signed-shift correlation with baseline entropy is zero---so on
gated arms the null rests on the temperature-matched design and the behavioral nulls, not on the ANCOVA.

\section{Related work}
\citet{teplica2025sciurus} is the closest work: shared uncertainty/factuality circuits across eight models
via causal tracing and zero-ablation. Relative to it we add the gated-twin data standard, twin-based
activation patching (reference-free counterfactuals), faithfulness against size-matched random sets,
single-neuron granularity inside the circuit, and the cross-type overlap measurement---and we inherit its
lesson that uncertainty machinery is not modular. \citet{zhao2026wired} localize confidence-mover
components for inflated numeric verbalized confidence (two models, faithfulness $>85$\%, no random-set
baselines); \citet{roy2025unwanted} mask uncertainty heads driving answer-flipping;
\citet{vazhentsev2026heads} use head patterns for hallucination detection; \citet{basu2025extractiveqa}
build context-vs-memory circuits; \citet{arora2026sparse} show task circuits sparse in the neuron basis
(MLP neurons only). Directions and features: \citet{ferrando2025iknow,anthropic2025biology,ji2025hedging}.
Neuron taxonomy: \citet{stolfo2024confidence,gurnee2024universal,du2025posttraining}. Ground truth:
\citet{kadavath2022know,gekhman2024slick}.

\section{Limitations}
Both cross-model circuit cells are contested arms with near-zero free-generation hedging; \qw{}'s
behavioral arm is too small to test (itself a finding: the model rarely expresses lack-of-knowledge
distributionally or behaviorally); the faithfulness criterion was fixed in code before the held-out runs
but not independently registered; the hedge readout is a token log-odds proxy validated against
free-generation hedging on the familiarity arm only; the ablation reference for gated arms
(pooled controls) overlaps the evaluation distribution; two mid-size instruct models.
