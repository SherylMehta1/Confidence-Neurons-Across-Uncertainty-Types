# What the 5-7 Oct experiments establish: claim-by-claim status for the UniReps draft

Every claim below was tested under a criterion written and hashed before its run, unless it is marked descriptive. Details and caveats are in each `FINDINGS.md`. An independent audit of the first five write-ups found 8 errors and 10 overstatements; all are corrected.

## Established (use in the paper)

| # | Claim | Evidence | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **In Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, saying "I don't know" follows how familiar the entity is more than whether the model knows the fact.** | Two judges, 96-token replies: popularity +0.39 vs knowledge +0.09, difference **+0.30 [+0.20, +0.40]**. Same sign under every instrument (+0.13 to +0.44). Survives name-spelling, token-rarity and guessable-answer controls. | `results/hedge_measure_v2/`, `results/unireps_checks/` |
| 2 | **Answer uncertainty (first-token entropy) follows knowledge, not familiarity, in all four families** (Llama, Qwen, Gemma, Mistral). | Knowledge effect ≥ 4× popularity effect; in Qwen popularity has no effect at all. No detector involved. | `results/factorial_4models/` |
| 3 | **Steering along the obscure-vs-popular direction makes the instruct model say "I don't know" about facts it knows; the knowledge direction does much less** (causal, behavioural, two families). **Qualified 7 Oct:** a familiarity direction fitted only on never-hedged items has a small effect (+0.04 [+0.01, +0.07] over random vs +0.27 for the full direction), so the effect is largely carried by hedge-associated components, not familiarity per se (`results/direction_nohedge/`). | Llama 0.02 → 0.29 (knowledge direction 0.09; Δ +0.20 [+0.14, +0.27]). Gemma-2-9B 0 → 0.22 (knowledge direction 0; Δ +0.22 [+0.16, +0.29]). Random directions: no effect. | `results/direction_reuse_v2/` |
| 4 | **Full intervention-level double dissociation in Gemma-2-9B-it:** the familiarity direction moves hedging, the knowledge direction moves entropy. | Hedging Δ +0.22 [+0.16, +0.29]; entropy Δ +0.68 [+0.59, +0.77]. In Llama only the hedging half holds. | `results/direction_reuse_v2/` |
| 5 | **A familiarity signal in the decoded "can't answer" readout is already present in base models** (Llama, Gemma, OLMo). | Jacobian lens, all four base models, mid-depth CIs above 0, on two different readouts. | `results/jlens_cascade/` |
| 5b | **The base-model familiarity signal survives name-spelling controls** (exploratory item-level regression, all four base models, both lens readouts). | Popularity coefficients unchanged with surface controls; every CI excludes 0. | `results/jlens_cascade/FINDINGS.md` |
| 6 | **The paper's lens timing holds after fixing a doubled-BOS input** (hedge half-formed by L16, entropy gap by L28, Llama familiarity). | Audit reproduces the committed numbers exactly, then shows identical layers with a single BOS. | `results/jlens_bos_audit/` |

## Tested and negative (report as such)

| Claim | Result |
|---|---|
| Familiarity is decoded *before* knowledge in depth (the human feeling-of-knowing "cascade") | **Mixed** across 8 models. The two signals mostly appear at the same layer, around 0.6 of depth, and the order depends on the readout. |
| Instruction tuning reuses the base model's familiarity direction | **Not supported.** The base direction does not steer the instruct model (v2, 2 pairs), and base/instruct alignment is no higher than for arbitrary item splits (v1 4 of 5 pairs; v2 2 of 2). Caveat: base directions were fitted on a different prompt format. |
| The dissociation in hedging behaviour generalises to "language models" | **Not licensed.** Qwen, Gemma and Mistral rarely say "I don't know" outright, and two judges cannot agree on their hedges (κ 0.30-0.47), so their comparison is unmeasured. The title stays scoped to Llama. |
| The circuit's first-token log-odds recovery reflects behaviour | **No.** The first-token score transfers fully even when generation does not change; the first-token readout mixes familiarity and knowledge (Llama +2.93 vs +3.06). |

## Withdrawn

- "Qwen, Gemma and Mistral are confabulators" (an artefact of the regex on truncated replies). Replace with: "rarely say they do not know; sometimes ask for clarification".
- Any claim that OLMo's lens anomaly is or is not about architecture.
