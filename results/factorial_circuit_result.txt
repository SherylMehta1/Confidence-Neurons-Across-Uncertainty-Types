2. THE DOC'S EXPECTED DISCRIMINATING PATTERN DID NOT OCCUR
================================================================================

The doc framed the read as: "If the circuit tracks knowledge (transfers on know_given_hi,
doesn't on pop_given_unknown) or the reverse, that's the mechanistic answer."

Neither happened. BOTH contrasts transfer, and the ordering runs OPPOSITE to the
knowledge hypothesis -- pop_given_unknown recovers MORE than know_given_hi:
  c->u difference +0.159, 95% CI [-0.019, +0.338]  (does not exclude 0)
  u->c difference +0.193, 95% CI [+0.033, +0.357]  (excludes 0)
(unpaired bootstrap; the two contrasts SHARE the popular_unknown cell, so they are not
independent samples and this comparison is approximate -- it is a descriptive contrast,
not a test.)

So step 5 does NOT deliver the knowledge-vs-popularity mechanistic answer it was meant to.
Reason, diagnosed below: neither runnable cell is actually a clean knowledge-vs-popularity
manipulation. Both collapse substantially into hedge-vs-no-hedge.

4. THE REAL QUALIFICATION: WHAT IS ACTUALLY IN EACH SLOT
================================================================================

Sampled hedge_rate composition of the held-out slots:
                      uncertain slot            control slot
know_given_hi         57.3% hedge==0            86.7% hedge==0
                      26.7% hedge>=0.8           1.3% hedge>=0.8
pop_given_unknown      5.5% hedge==0            67.0% hedge==0
                      78.9% hedge>=0.8          17.4% hedge>=0.8

(a) know_given_hi's "uncertain" slot is MAJORITY CONFABULATOR. 57.3% of popular_unknown
    held-out prompts never hedge, and 61.3% of pairs have a hedge gap <= 0. So for most
    pairs this contrast is "knows it" vs "doesn't know it and states a wrong answer
    anyway" -- not a hedge contrast at all. Splitting on that:

      direction             |hedge gap|>=0.1        |hedge gap|<0.1
      control_to_uncertain  +0.883 (n=29)           +0.686 (n=46)
      uncertain_to_control  +0.934 (n=29)           +0.750 (n=46)

    know_given_hi's pass is CARRIED BY THE MINORITY (29/75) that has a real behavioural
    hedge gap. On the 46 zero-gap pairs the c->u direction falls BELOW 0.7 (+0.686). Its
    c->u bootstrap CI lower bound is also +0.610, i.e. below 0.7 -- it passes on the bare
    sample mean, which is the convention used for familiarity and locked for U/U, but it
    would not clear a CI-based bar. Treat know_given_hi as the weaker of the two results.

(b) pop_given_unknown is, in substance, a HEDGER-vs-CONFABULATOR contrast: 78.9% of the
    uncertain slot hedges >=0.8 while 67% of the control slot never hedges, with both
    sides SliCK-Unknown. That is the U/U design -- reached by sorting on popularity
    instead of by selecting on hedge rate directly. Its recovery holds in both the
    gap>=0.1 (n=89) and gap<0.1 (n=20) subsets (+0.898/+1.011 and +1.022/+1.030), so it
    is not carried by a subgroup.

5. THE MOST USEFUL THING THIS RUN PRODUCED (reframed, and exploratory)
================================================================================

Because of 4(b), pop_given_unknown functions as a much larger, independently constructed
corroboration of the U/U falsification test, on 109 held-out pairs instead of 23:

                        U/U (pre-registered, n=23)       pop_given_unknown (n=109)
  c->u                  +1.020  CI [+0.735, +1.284]      +0.921  CI [+0.819, +1.022]
  u->c                  +0.703  CI [+0.374, +0.998]      +1.014  CI [+0.913, +1.113]
  random null (c->u)    +0.182 +- 0.071                  +0.050 +- 0.044

The U/U confirmatory run passed its locked bar but, as recorded in
step_f_confirmatory_result.txt, the injection direction cleared 0.7 by 0.003 -- inside the
band c034557 pre-declared as underpowered. pop_given_unknown lands that same direction at
+1.014 with a CI whose lower bound (+0.913) is far clear of 0.7, against a near-zero null.

IMPORTANT LIMIT ON THIS CLAIM: this reframing is POST HOC. pop_given_unknown was built and
run as a popularity contrast, not as a U/U replication; nothing about it was
pre-registered, and the hedger/confabulator composition in 4(b) was discovered after the
result was in hand. It is corroborative exploratory evidence that strengthens confidence in
the U/U finding. It is NOT a second confirmatory test and must not be reported as one, and
it does not retroactively resolve U/U's underpowered direction. Also note the two share the
popular_unknown cell with know_given_hi and draw from the same familiarity_v3 screen, so
"independent" means independently constructed, not an independent sample.

6. WHAT TO CARRY INTO STEP 8
================================================================================
1. The hedging circuit transfers to BOTH runnable factorial cells on held-out pairs, 20
   heads (1.95%) + 100 neurons (0.022%), against near-zero random nulls.
2. The factorial does NOT answer whether the circuit tracks knowledge or popularity. The
   two confounded cells were undiagnosable from the start; the two runnable cells both
   reduce largely to hedge-vs-no-hedge. Do not claim a knowledge-vs-popularity mechanistic
   dissociation from this.
3. know_given_hi (+0.762/+0.821) passes on bare means but is carried by 29/75 pairs with a
   real hedge gap and has a c->u CI lower bound of +0.610. Report with that qualification.
4. pop_given_unknown (+0.921/+1.014) is the strong, robust result, and its value is as
   post-hoc corroboration of U/U, not as a popularity finding.
5. What would actually test knowledge-vs-popularity: a re-screen that holds hedge rate
   matched across cells, so the manipulation is not confounded with the outcome. That is
   new work, not a fix to this step.
