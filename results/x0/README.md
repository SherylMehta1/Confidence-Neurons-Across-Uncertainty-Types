## Where the X0 plan needs correcting

1. **+0.13 is not "+0.30 with one judge".** Qwen alone on the 96-token replies gives +0.44. The +0.13 uses 24-token replies, another prompt and a regex correction, which raises popular unknown abstention to 0.57 (0.22 on long replies). `A4_margin_steps.csv`
2. **"Corrected" means two things.** Judge-corrected (+0.13) and label-corrected (+0.18 gap, +0.14 margin). Step 10's formula defines only the first.
3. **"1 in 12 labels unstable" is arithmetic.** 14 of 87 obscure known facts sit at 9 of 10 and half of their random halves miss; the unknown 100% is true by construction. Paraphrased prompt: 27 of 87 stay known, 49 drop to the band, 11 become unknown.
4. **The greedy answer is stored.** With the paper's rule: 801 / 763 / 707 known at 8 / 9 / 10 of 10 and 1,540 unknown (plan: 803 and 1,543). Margin +0.28 [+0.17, +0.38] at 10 of 10.
5. **"Popularity carries more weight than entropy" (A7) holds only across arms.** Relation-resampled interval of the difference [-0.02, +0.16]; within each arm entropy is the larger predictor.
6. **The 0.25 in A3 is near a lower bound.** In 26 of 87 obscure known questions a gold answer is in the name. On the other 61: 0.33 [0.23, 0.44].
7. **kappa is lowest in obscure unknown (0.36), but that cell cancels out of the margin.** The two cells the margin uses have 0.56 and 0.59. It does matter for the unknown-fact gap.
8. **Not every interval uses 10,000 draws.** Relation resampling and the judge correction use 5,000, linear probability models 2,000.
